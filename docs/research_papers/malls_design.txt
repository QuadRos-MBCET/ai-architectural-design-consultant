Architectural Guidelines for Shopping Malls:
1. Spatial Organization: Malls require massive footprints with anchor stores at the extremities to ensure foot traffic across smaller retail zones.
2. Circulation: Corridors should be a minimum of 6 meters wide. Use a looped or figure-eight layout to prevent dead ends.
3. Daylighting: Incorporate large central atrium skylights (with Low-E glazing) to provide natural light to interior circulation spaces.
4. MEP Systems: Centralized HVAC systems with high-capacity chillers are required. Exhaust systems must be routed to the roof.
5. Sustainability: Incorporate vast solar canopies over parking areas and rainwater harvesting systems for landscape irrigation.

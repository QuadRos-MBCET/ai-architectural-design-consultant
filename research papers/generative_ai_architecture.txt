Generative AI in Architecture (VAE, GAN, Diffusion):
1. Variational Autoencoders (VAE): Used to compress architectural floor plan graphs into a latent space. By sampling from this continuous latent space, architects can instantly generate smooth interpolations between different building massings or room layouts.
2. Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN): Often used for generating highly realistic conceptual renders or translating 2D floor plans into 3D voxel models. GANs consist of a generator (inventing the design) and a discriminator (ensuring the design is architecturally valid).
3. Diffusion Models: The state-of-the-art in generative design. Diffusion models (like Stable Diffusion, TRELLIS, or Hunyuan3D) can generate completely novel 3D meshes and text-to-image architectural renders. In this RAG pipeline, the structured JSON specifications are used to construct base procedural models, which can then be used as ControlNet depth-maps or structural bounds for a Diffusion model to generate the final photorealistic architectural concept.
